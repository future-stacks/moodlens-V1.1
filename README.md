# 🧠 MoodLens

**MoodLens** is a habit-support tool for high-functioning individuals who struggle with overthinking, mental overload, and staying consistent.

It transforms your current state into a **clear, immediate action** — helping you maintain momentum instead of getting stuck in analysis.

---

## ⚡ Core Principle

> **Action > Reflection**

MoodLens is designed to convert emotional noise into a **usable signal** in under 60 seconds.

---

## 🚀 Core Features

### 🔁 Mood → Action Engine
Powered by **Future&Hope**, users input their current state (e.g., *Anxious*, *Stuck*, *Overwhelmed*) and receive a structured **Micro-Intervention**:

- **Micro-Action** → < 2 minutes, designed to break inertia  
- **Cognitive Reframe** → reduces resistance  
- **Next Step** → removes ambiguity  
- *(Optional)* Reflection prompt  

---

### ⏱ The 2-Minute Rule
Every intervention is intentionally constrained to **≤ 120 seconds**.

This lowers activation energy and helps users:
- start faster  
- avoid overthinking  
- build consistency through repetition  

---

### 🧠 Cognitive Reframing Layer
Instead of treating emotions as blockers, MoodLens reframes them as:

> **Signals that require a small, specific response**

---

### 📈 Streak & Momentum System
A lightweight consistency tracker designed to be:

- **Forgiving** → encourages recovery after missed days  
- **Firm** → maintains accountability  

Focus: **momentum, not perfection**

---

### 🧾 History & Pattern Recognition
- Persistent log of past interventions  
- Helps users identify:
  - recurring states  
  - effective actions  
- Builds self-trust over time  

---

### 🔔 Smart Reminders
- Custom daily check-ins  
- Browser notifications  
- Designed to reduce reliance on memory or motivation  

---

## 🎨 Design Philosophy

MoodLens uses an **Atmospheric / Immersive UI**:

- Deep dark theme  
- Subtle radial glows  
- Glassmorphism elements  
- Smooth micro-interactions  

The goal is to create a **calm, focused “mission control” environment** — not another noisy productivity app.

---

## 🧠 Product Insight

Most productivity tools fail high-performers because they:

- add more tasks  
- increase cognitive load  
- rely on motivation  

MoodLens does the opposite.

> It reduces the cost of starting.

By outsourcing decision-making (what to do next) to the system, users can:
- stop thinking  
- start acting  
- maintain momentum

## 🔄 Example Intervention

**Input:** *“Overwhelmed”*

**Output:**
- **Micro-Action:** Write down the 3 smallest tasks you can complete right now  
- **Reframe:** You don’t need to solve everything — reduce the load  
- **Next Step:** Start with the easiest task immediately  

---

## ⚠️ Design Constraints

- No long-form journaling  
- No heavy gamification  
- No complex workflows  
- No dependency on motivation  

Everything is optimized for:
> **speed, clarity, and execution**
